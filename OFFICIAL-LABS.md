## Discovery policy

- Scan organization repositories and relevant releases; identify linked companion repositories and creators.
- Deduplicate by canonical repository URL and track the original maintainers.
- Store project category, description, source organization, license/status, latest activity, and Voshu relevance.
- Promote only reviewed discoveries to front-page highlights.
- Preserve existing INSPO inventory and manually curated notes.
